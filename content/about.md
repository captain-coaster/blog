# About Captain Coaster

Captain Coaster is a participative roller coaster guide. Riders look up coasters and parks, rate and review the ones they've ridden, build their Top lists and follow a world ranking calculated from these contributions.

## Our Goal

We want Captain Coaster to be a companion for riders: a place to find a coaster, keep track of what you've ridden and share your passion. Everyone can search and read. An account lets you contribute, one step at a time: mark a coaster as ridden, rate it, then review it if you feel like it.

## What You Can Do

- **Search**: Find a coaster by its name or its park's name, then read its information, reviews and photos
- **Follow the World Ranking**: See how coasters compare, with a ranking updated every month from members' ratings and Tops
- **Rate and Review**: Share your opinion on the coasters you've ridden
- **Build Your Tops**: Put your favorite coasters in order and shape the ranking with more precision
- **Explore the Map**: Find coasters near you or plan your next park visit

## The Ranking

The credibility of the ranking comes from its method: only riders who have experienced both coasters weigh in on a comparison between them. You can read how it works in [Understanding Captain Coaster's Ranking System](/blog/understanding-captain-coaster-ranking-system).

## Our Community

Everything on Captain Coaster rests on what riders contribute. As of October 2026, that means:

- 880,000+ ratings
- 100,000+ reviews
- 26,000+ riders

## Development

Captain Coaster is built and maintained by a small team of roller coaster fans who are also web developers. It's available in English, French, Spanish and German, and it keeps evolving with feedback from its members.
